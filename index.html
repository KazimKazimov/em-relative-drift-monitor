Where the edge lives · per-unit PnL by entry state (analog rule, before costs)

Each trade is booked at one unit of factor-hedged notional and attributed to the state it was entered in. Mean is bp per trade over the full hold; t is mean / standard error. The path chart averages the cumulative PnL by day in trade: a curve that flattens early says the edge is in the first days after entry and the holding period is too long; one that keeps rising says the move persists past the horizon.

Net score g_i = mean_j (S_ij − S_ji)/2, positive = cheapening vs peers. With 1/β sizing each leg is scaled by the inverse of its recursive beta at the signal date, so the long and short sides carry equal factor exposure (net β = 0 ex ante) and PnL is on raw yield changes in bp, gross notional held equal to the equal-weight book. The equal-weight option instead books each name against its own factor hedge and credits the residual. Signal at close t, PnL on t+1. Warm-up 60 days.
